This installs HEIC Converter, a background helper that watches your Downloads folder and automatically converts .heic images to PNG, JPG, or both.

Converted images are written next to the originals. Nothing is deleted, moved, or uploaded — conversion happens entirely on this Mac using the image decoder built into macOS.

What gets installed

  • HEIC Converter in your Applications folder, for changing settings
  • The watcher and its control command, in /usr/local
  • A background agent that starts when you log in
  • A settings file in your Library folder

One step can’t be automated. After installing, macOS still needs your permission for a background task to read your Downloads folder. The final screen explains how to grant it — it takes about thirty seconds, and conversion will not work until you do.
